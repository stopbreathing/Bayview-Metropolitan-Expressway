# Bayview Metropolitan Expressway (BME) Project

[![Project Status: Alpha/Planning](https://shields.io)](https://github.com)
[![Platform: NFS Underground 2](https://shields.io)](https://github.com)

Welcome to the official development repository of the **Bayview Metropolitan Expressway Co., Ltd.** — a comprehensive world-space reconstruction, scale correction, and map extension framework for *Need for Speed: Underground 2*.

Our division is dedicated to re-engineering the structural topology of Bayview, expanding its regional connectivity, and restoring long-lost civil infrastructure.

---

## 📐 Project Scope & Infrastructure Overview

The primary objective of the **Regional Highway Link & Connectors** initiative is to overhaul the spatial layout of Bayview and physically bridge it with Olympic City (*NFS Underground 1*), creating a singular, seamless metropolitan ecosystem.

### 🌐 1. Inter-City Highway Connectors (UG1 🔗 UG2)
*   **Objective:** Designing and implementing a massive, high-detail highway and bridge network.
*   **Technical Details:** Physical integration of the Olympic City terrain into the Bayview stream data, establishing seamless high-speed transits between both maps without loading screens.

### 🔍 2. Metropolitan Scale Correction & Asset Overhaul
*   **Objective:** Restoring realistic architectural and environmental proportions.
*   **Technical Details:** Total redesign of low-poly background assets, rescaling oversized organic models (e.g., corporate palm trees), and recalibrating miniature road signs to meet proper 1:1 driving simulator standards.

### 🏟️ 3. Isolated Infrastructure Integration
*   **Objective:** Embedding closed racing circuits into the open-world geometry.
*   **Technical Details:** Relocating and hard-linking hidden maps (Bayview Stadium, Street-X circuits, and Drift arenas) directly into the main free-roam world coordinate system.

### 💾 4. Legacy Structural Restoration (Beta Content)
*   **Objective:** Reconstruction of abandoned civic projects from the 2004 pre-release builds.
*   **Technical Details:** Extracting, cleaning, and compiling cut highways, unused intersections, and early layout splines discovered within the retail game files.

### 🏜️ 5. The Desert Drive Expansion
*   **Objective:** Full from-scratch construction of the legendary cut desert region.
*   **Technical Details:** Building a brand-new environmental sector connected to the outer highway ring, expanding the total drivable area of the game.
